How We Did It

Four steps to clarity

01 · Collect

Every RR. Every department.

Pulled Recruitment Rules, pay scales, exam schemes, and group classifications from 44 departments and 101 establishments across Maharashtra.

02 · Standardise

One schema. One truth.

Mapped every cadre to a unified standard — classifying under cadre families for rationalising the recruitment examination, replacing inconsistent formats with a single source of truth.

03 · Compare

Filter. Search. Compare.

Enabled the first-ever side-by-side comparison of any two cadres — with multi-filter drill-down, full-text search, RR analysis charts, and Word/Excel export.

04 · Generate

Draft new RRs from the standard.

Generate fresh Recruitment Rules directly from the standardised cadre data — using cadre profiles, exam bucket classification, and pay scale mapping as the foundation.
